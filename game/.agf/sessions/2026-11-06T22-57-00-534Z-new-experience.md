**What's in the game now**
- **Home (title screen):** matches `target_home.png`. It has the logo, the raider bursting through two defenders (with idle motion), coins and Chant Tokens, a 7-day daily reward, chapter progress, one big "Play · Trial N", and Trials / Matches / Shop / Settings buttons.
- **Trials map:** 66 trials in 6 chapter bands, with stars and crowned chapter bosses.
- **Match screen:** a Skia-drawn mat in each chapter's style (mud, clay, then royal blue) with the midline, baulk and bonus lines. The scoreboard has crest shields, the clock and the 30 s raid ring, a goal strip, and a pause button and Coach button.
  - **Raiding:** tap a defender, then pick a touch move, then pick a way home. Each move button shows the engine's real risk ("20% held").
  - **Defending:** the CPU's move is shown in advance. Tap any of your defenders to select him, then pick a hold; each hold shows its real grip chance.
  - **Overlays:** struggle, DRS review, choosing which raider goes, and the auction hall. A referee panel lists each raid's events, and the scoreboard adds up those same events.
  - **Ending:** wins play a staged celebration before the result card; losses show a fail card with the reason.
- **Matches hub:**
  - Quick Match (Easy / Medium / Hard, Blitz or Full).
  - Tournament and Championship brackets, with lock cards "Complete 1 match (0/1)" and "Win 1 Tournament (0/1)".
  - The Daily Ghost, which opens after Trial 5.
  - The Online Raid Race: find a rival or use a friend room by code. Both players play the same seeded match, see each other's live score, and can send the 8 fixed emotes. It falls back to an AI rival when nobody is found or you're offline.
- **Shop and Settings:**
  - Shop: Remove Ads, coin packs (store prices only), Coach hints for 150 coins, and an optional ad for coins.
  - Settings: sound, music, vibration, left-handed layout, reduced motion, reminders, Share and Restore purchases.
- **Other:**
  - A tutorial hand guides Trials 1, 2 and 4.
  - Android back works on every screen instead of leaving the app.
  - The analytics events and the notification calls are wired in.
  - There are 6 store screenshot scenes, each a real engine state reached by playing seeded moves. They are home, raid, defend, auction, map and superraid; the win screen isn't one of them.

**Decisions and gaps to know about**
- **Portrait, not landscape:** the GDD asks for landscape, but the art targets and backgrounds are portrait, so I kept the portrait setting in `app.json`.
- **Baked art:** I made move buttons in each move's colour, separate coin and token icons, and a saffron-kit defender for your team from the existing sprites (`scripts/art/bake.py`).
- **Timing is a choice, not a skill:** tackle and struggle timing are shown as animations, but success is decided by the move you pick, not by when you tap.
- **Not built yet (left for stage 3):** the Kit Album, jersey customiser, Season Pass, Roadmap and commentary. Coins can currently only be spent on Coach hints.
- **Web-preview quirk:** popups render 500 px wide on web, so they look shifted there. That's a preview artifact; I didn't treat it as a device bug.

No factory or tooling problems came up this session.
