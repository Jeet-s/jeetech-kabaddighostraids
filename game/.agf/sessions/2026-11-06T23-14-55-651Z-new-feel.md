**Match feel**
- **Every move:**
  - The athlete squashes and pops.
  - Sand dust kicks up at his feet, with speed streaks on Dash, Rush, Lunge and Dubki.
  - Tapping a defender gives a small burst on him.
- **Contact:** touches, holds and tackles slam in the gold starburst art. Super Tackles get a bigger one plus dust.
- **Scoring:** points fly up into the scoreboard shields as "+N". The referee panel's calls now slide in one by one, so a multi-point raid reads call by call.
- **Raid clock:** the badge ticks with a punch whenever the clock moves, and turns crimson and throbs in the last 5 s.
- **Ghost raiders:** they now trail teal afterimages.
- **Choice gems:** each new row springs up out of the bar.
- **Phase cards:**
  - Struggle, DRS, Who Raids and the auction ease in.
  - The auction card punches on each new lot or raised price.
  - The DRS tablet plays a beep and a scan sweep.
- **Win:** the sequence is now staged: a VICTORY / TRIAL WON banner with the score, the whole side jumps, two waves of confetti from the mat, then the win card.
- **Loss:** a shake and a DEFEAT / TRIAL LOST banner with the reason, then the fail card.

**Menus**
- The Matches hub cards slide in one after another. A locked card shakes before its lock card opens.
- The trials map opens by gliding up to your current trial.
- Home has floodlight embers drifting behind the hero.

**New: Kit Album** (Home → Album)
- Until now coins could only buy Coach hints, so I added a real place to spend them.
- It has 12 fixed cosmetics in 3 sets: crest shields, raid trails and raid banners. They cost coins or Chant Tokens, and nothing is random.
- Each item shows in your next match: your scoreboard shield, your raid path on the mat, and your raid and victory banners.
- A complete set pays a one-time bonus.
- `store/FACTS.md`, `docs/CONTENT.md` and `game.config.json` now list it.
