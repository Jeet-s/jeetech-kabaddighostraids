I fixed render_defects and game_feel at the root and checked them in a temporary web preview, but level_design still fails: the last two self-review rolls scored 6.8 and 7 out of 10 (bar 8). All the required checks pass: `npx tsc --noEmit` has 0 errors, `npm run pitfalls` reports 0/0, `npm run validate` shows 66/66 solvable, `npm run levels` passes, and `npm run playtest` meets all 11 pacing rules. **Rendering (shared components, not one screen)**
- **TOUCHED tag:** it was an unsized label over the defender's torso, so it wrapped to "TOUCHE / D". Touched defenders now get a small ✓ chip in the number badge above the head, plus a gold ring at their feet.
- **Win card ghosting:** the shared Popup card faded in from 20% opacity, so the board showed through it. Every popup card is now solid from its first frame; only its size and position animate, and the backdrop is darker.
- **Banner cut off at the edge:** banners used to slide across the screen. They now slam in where they stand and fade out.
- **Referee card covering defenders:** it now sits inside the bottom control bar, in the space the move buttons leave while a raid resolves.
- **Legend over the midline:** it moved to a row at the top of the bar.
- **Defenders vanishing:** defenders who are out now walk off toward their side during the referee's call instead of disappearing between frames.
- **Raider overlapping defenders:** between touches the raider steps back toward the baulk line, clear of the front row.

**Game feel**
- **The "…" dead time is gone.** While a move resolves, the chosen move button stays up and glowing, with a line saying what is happening ("Hand Touch on #4…", "Dashing for the midline!", then "You score +1!").
- **Stronger tap response:** move buttons squash further on press.
- **Defender reactions:** a hit defender flashes white and gets knocked back.
- **Return animation:** a trail is drawn to the midline when the raider heads home.
- **Win sequence:** the board fades to a dark backdrop first. Then the card opens with gold rays behind it, each star punches and shakes the card, and coins rain into the count-up.
- **Briefing card:** trimmed to the goal, a short "Lose if" line, the stars and one coach line. Start flies the card off before play begins.
- **Rewarded ad (feature tour warning):** the Shop's "Free 50 coins" now always shows a visible result, including when no video is ready.

Three new problems came up in the preview and are fixed: the end-of-trial darkening was drawn underneath the defenders, the glow ring crossed the move button's label, and the defence prompt still said "pick a defender" after one was selected.
