I've fixed the feature-tour blocker and done a different, root-cause pass on rendering, game feel and levels. One thing is still short of the bar: the factory level review now scores 6.4/10 and exits non-zero (bar is 8). Its weak list was mostly different units from last round. `tsc`, `pitfalls` (0 errors), `validate` (66/66 solvable, curve 3.2×) and `npm run levels` all pass, and every playtest pacing gate passes (last-third casual 64%).

**Feature tour (the non-quality blocker)**
- **Season Pass purchase path:** `monetization.json` has no pass SKU, so I added a Gold Track. It costs a fixed 1200 in-game coins and adds a gold reward to all 10 tiers, back-paid for tiers already claimed. A "Get coins" button opens the store's coin pack, or shows "store not reachable". The Shop has a new "Season Pass · Gold Track" row that opens the pass.
- **Double coins:** the button on the win card used to appear only when a video was already loaded, which is why the tester couldn't find it. It now always shows after a win and says "No video ready yet" when there isn't one.

**Render and game-feel root causes**
- **Buttons looked dead on press:** the pressed button was swapped out for a "move in progress" copy in the same frame, so its press effect was thrown away. That copy now starts already flashing, with rings, a punch and a gold halo.
- **Scoreboard looked stale:** points only count once the raider gets home, and the HUD showed nothing before that. A "+N LIVE" chip now sits under my shield and the strip reads "Points 0/2 (+2 live)". The "+N" meant to fly to the scoreboard never got there, because every effect's text was being moved to the middle of the mat; that's fixed.
- **Win button "jitter":** this was the Next button's breathing scale. It's replaced by a glow behind the button, so its size never changes.
- **Static win screen with an empty bottom:** the light rays now turn slowly for about 30 s. The card has a stats row that ticks in (score, points, raids, caught) and a "Next up" card for the next trial. Scoring a raid now gets a burst on the midline, a screen shake and confetti.
- **Tutorial hand covering defenders and flipping poses:** replaced by a bouncing "TAP ▼" tag above the target's number badge. For buttons, the button's own label lights up gold instead.
- **"BAULK" cut to "BAU":** court line labels now move to the side away from the raider and sit on a dark plate above the players.
- **Raider pulled back up the mat:** when held on the way home, he was drawn up next to a back-row holder. He now stays short of the midline.

I checked all of this in a temporary web build (since removed). The tap tag, "+2 LIVE", the line labels, the full win card with a stable button width (160 px across frames) and the Season Pass messages all render as intended.

**Level design**
- **Kick:** it is no longer safe on every Rusher. A Rusher standing next to the kicked man now charges the raider's back, so a lone Rusher is still the safe kick but a pair isn't. Kick buttons and the solver's READ rows now show the kick's chance to land.
- **Ghost duels:** the mat now shows the ghost's same-numbered raid, its moves and result, replayed from the real recording, so it's no longer a static number. The struggle odds now appear for 0, 1 and 2 touches.
- **Redesigned by hand:** units 14, 16, 24, 27, 34, 41, 43, 47 (replaces the repeat of 26), 48, 49, 51, 62 and 63, all re-tuned. Intents were rewritten from the measured numbers, including 21, 32, 53, 54, 65 and 66, and `docs/CONTENT.md` and `store/FACTS.md` are updated.
- **Left for later:** 50 (rigged DRS calls), 30 (scouting introduced inside a match), 35 (specialist bonus too small) and a player-built Ghost Gate.
